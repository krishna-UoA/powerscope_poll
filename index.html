Research survey · Power-system education

How should simulation help us teach and learn power systems?

Fault currents, rotor swings and the frequency dip after a generator trip are hard to show on slides. We are evaluating PowerScope, a free browser-based simulator that explains why the system behaves as it does. Your answers will shape the next version and its evaluation.

▶ Try PowerScope first ↗

💡 Animations not moving? The PowerScope animations (rotating wind turbines and generators) may not play because of your computer's power-saving or motion settings. Windows: Settings → Accessibility → Visual effects → Animation effects: On. Mac: System Settings → Accessibility → Display → Reduce motion: Off.
